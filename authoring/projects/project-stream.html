PUBLIC RESEARCH CASE STUDY · AGENTIC AI

Context engineering in Project Stream.

Managing the cost and latency of growing conversation history in enterprise content creation workflows.

The setting

Adobe’s Project Stream is the production-scale agentic AI platform evaluated in my IEEE RECCAP 2026 paper. The work examines how context-management choices affect automated content creation workflows.

My contribution

My contributions to agentic content creation at Adobe informed the research presented in Context Optimization Techniques for Production-Scale Multi-Agent Enterprise Applications. The paper evaluates three complementary strategies using LangChain and LangGraph.

The architectural choices

  • Semantic summarization to compress accumulated context.
  • Relevance-based context editing to filter information for the task.
  • Multi-layer adaptive caching to reduce redundant work.

Evaluating the trade-offs

Reducing context is useful only if the system continues to produce acceptable results. The evaluation therefore considers token costs, response latency, and output quality together.

Across more than 10,000 real-world requests over three months, the paper reports 60% lower token costs, a 40% improvement in end-to-end response latency, and less than 3% degradation in output quality compared with the baseline.

What to take away

Context management is an architectural decision that touches agent roles, workflow state, and evaluation. Summarization, editing, and caching address different sources of overhead and should be assessed together against real tasks.

This case study is based on the published paper’s public abstract. It does not include internal implementation details or claim that these results apply to every deployment.
