Who is responsible

Abani Behera operates this personal website. It is not an Adobe corporate website. For privacy questions or requests, contact me through my LinkedIn profile. Please do not include sensitive information in an initial message.

Browsing and website delivery

When you request a page, the services delivering this website receive technical information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time. Hosting and delivery providers may process connection data and keep operational or security logs to deliver and protect the website. Rejecting analytics does not prevent this necessary connection processing.

Optional Google Analytics

With your permission, this site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and improve its content. Depending on the measurement settings, information can include pages visited, referral sources, visit times, approximate location, browser and device information, and interactions such as scrolling, outbound clicks, or file downloads. Analytics identifiers can distinguish browsers and sessions.

The site does not load the Google Analytics script until you select Accept analytics, or return with a saved acceptance. Choosing Reject analytics lets you continue browsing without this site's Google Analytics tracking. Visits and actions before acceptance are not measured by this integration.

The analytics integration keeps advertising storage, advertising user data, and advertising personalization consent denied. Google signals and advertising-personalization signals are disabled in the site's tag configuration. The initial page URL and referrer supplied by the site omit query strings and fragments; other automatic measurements depend on the property's Google Analytics settings.

Google processes analytics information to provide the service. Processing may take place outside your country, subject to Google's applicable terms and safeguards. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies and browser storage

  • analytics-consent: browser local storage used to remember whether you accepted or rejected analytics. It has no automatic expiry in this site's code and remains until you change the choice or clear this site's browser data. If storage is blocked, the choice lasts only for the current page.
  • _ga and _ga_*: Google Analytics cookies created after acceptance to distinguish browsers and maintain session information. Google documents a default expiry of up to two years for these cookies; actual expiry and renewal depend on tag settings and browser restrictions.
  • Other preferences: site features may store preferences, such as a selected color scheme, separately from analytics consent.

Browser storage is specific to the browser and device you use. Clearing site data removes the saved choice, so you may be asked again.

Change your choice

Use Cookie settings at the bottom of the page to reopen the analytics controls, then choose Accept analytics or Reject analytics. The control appears on the production site where this integration is enabled.

Withdrawing consent disables further measurement through this integration and attempts to remove accessible Google Analytics cookies for this site. On subsequent pages, the saved rejection prevents the Google Analytics script from loading. Withdrawal does not recall data already sent, remove Google's already loaded code from the current page, or change cookies belonging to other websites. You can also remove site cookies and storage using your browser settings.

Speaking enquiries and external services

If you choose to open the speaking enquiry form, the site loads a Google Forms embed. Opening the form connects your browser to Google independently of your analytics choice. Google may process technical information or use storage under its own policies. A separate link lets you open the form directly on Google's website.

Information you choose to submit is used to review and respond to your enquiry and arrange any resulting engagement. Please provide only what is needed and do not send confidential or sensitive personal information. Form submissions are handled through Google Forms, not as Google Analytics events in this site's code.

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, publications, and other external websites take you to services governed by their own privacy notices. Your analytics choice here does not control those services.

How long information is kept

Analytics event and user data are retained according to the retention settings of this site's Google Analytics property. Cookie expiry is separate from server-side data retention, and aggregated reports may remain available longer. Hosting-log retention is controlled by the relevant delivery provider. Enquiry information is kept for handling the request and any resulting engagement, subject to applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact me if you need more information about retention or want to request deletion.

Your privacy requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information, withdraw consent, or raise a concern with a data protection authority. Contact me through LinkedIn to discuss a request. Please identify the service and approximate date involved without sending sensitive information. Analytics data may not identify you by name, so it may not be possible to locate a particular visit without additional information.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the site's services or practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest update.
