What I work on

My Agentic AI work includes multi-agent orchestration, context engineering, RAG, and secure enterprise architectures using LangChain, LangGraph, and Azure OpenAI. At Adobe, I contribute to agentic content creation workflows, connecting practical engineering with research on cost, latency, and security.

My broader background includes Adobe Experience Manager, Edge Delivery Services, digital asset management, API design, event-driven systems, and cloud-native application architecture.

Research and community
